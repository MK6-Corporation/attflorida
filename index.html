Part one

Natal, Rio Grande do Norte

April 20 – 26 · Vogal Luxury Beach Hotel & Spa
Day 1Tue · Apr 20

Arrival in Natal

Our local team welcomes the group at Natal International Airport, followed by a private transfer to the hotel. After check-in the rest of the day is yours: enjoy the hotel, walk along the beach or rest after the journey.

Private airport transfer
Day 2Wed · Apr 21

Natal City Tour & Forte dos Reis Magos

A guided tour through Natal's history, culture and main landmarks, including the Forte dos Reis Magos (Three Wise Men Fortress) at the entrance of the Potengi River, with wide views of the coastline.

Day 3Thu · Apr 22

South Coast & the World's Largest Cashew Tree

A full day along the South Coast of Rio Grande do Norte, known for its beaches, dramatic scenery and small coastal communities. A special stop at the World's Largest Cashew Tree, a single tree that covers an area the size of a city block.

Day 4Fri · Apr 23

Day at leisure

No scheduled tours. Relax at the hotel, enjoy the beach or explore the city on your own.

Free day
Day 5Sat · Apr 24

FEMPTUR, the Rio Grande do Norte Tourism Fair

A guided visit to FEMPTUR, where towns from across the state showcase their destinations, culture, gastronomy, handicrafts and heritage. Look for handmade bobbin lace and local crafts.

The fair is also a chance to taste the state's award-winning artisanal cheeses. Potiguar producers took 47 medals at the 9th Prêmio Queijo Brasil in 2026, including 16 golds, for coalho cheese, manteiga cheese and sertão butter. The rest of the day is free.

Day 6Sun · Apr 25

Barra do Cunhaú: coast & mangroves

A full-day excursion (about 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM) to Barra do Cunhaú, 92 km from Natal, where rivers meet the Atlantic among reefs, natural pools and mangroves. Lunch stop at a local seafood restaurant.

  • Included: transportation, licensed English-speaking guide, passenger insurance
  • Optional ecological boat tour through the mangroves: R$ 105 per person
  • Lunch and beverages are not included
Day 7Mon · Apr 26

Natal → Rio de Janeiro

A free morning, then a private transfer to Natal International Airport for the direct flight to Rio. On arrival, a private transfer with an English-speaking guide takes the group to the hotel in Copacabana. The rest of the day is free.

Transfers both ends
